*[micro-units]: The length of half a 2x microbrick. Is the smallest possible grid snap. A plate's height is 4 micro-units, a stud's length/width is 10 micro-units, and the height of a 1x1 brick is 12 micro-units.
*[Esc menu]: The game will still run in the background, even in singleplayer. So in this case I'm calling it a Esc menu. Not a pause menu.

*[gizmo]: Mouse-based control buttons in the 3D space for precision.
