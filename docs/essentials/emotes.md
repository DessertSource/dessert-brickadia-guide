# Emotes

You can perform gestures using the emote wheel by holding ++h++ and hovering over the emote you want to do.

![Emote wheel](../assets/media/essentials/emotes/emote-wheel.jpg)

Release ++h++ to do the selected emote.

Certain emotes may play sounds, or be persistent until you cancel them yourself.

## Cancelling emotes

This is mostly useful for persistent emotes like Sit or Hug.

Open the emote wheel by holding ++h++, and hover over the X icon.
Release ++h++ to cancel emotes.

![Emote wheel when cancelling an emote](../assets/media/essentials/emotes/emote-wheel-cancel.jpg)

Emotes also automatically cancel when you use another emote.
