# Looking around

By default, you are in first person view with your camera inside your head.

![First person view](../assets/media/essentials/looking-around/first-person-view.jpg)

To move your view in first person, move your mouse.

Your movement is relative to your look direction.

## Zoom

Hold ++z++ to zoom in and look at smaller details.

- Scroll up your mouse wheel to zoom in.
- Scroll down your mouse wheel to zoom out.

![Zooming](../assets/media/essentials/looking-around/zoom.jpg)

## Free cursor mode view

If you enable free cursor mode using ++m++, your view is locked to the current direction until you hold the ++rightbutton++ to rotate your view around.

## Flashlight

![Player using the flashlight](../assets/media/essentials/looking-around/flashlight.jpg)

The flashlight allows you to see in dark or shaded places. Toggle it using the ++f++ key.

## Third person view

![Third person view](../assets/media/essentials/looking-around/third-person-view.jpg)

Toggle between first person view and third person view using the ++tab++ key.

In third person view, your movement is relative to the direction of the camera.

### Disabling view transition animation

If you prefer the perspective switch to be instant, you can disable the view transition animation that you get when you switch to third person or first person.

To toggle the view transition animation:

1. Open the **:material-cog:{.button .item .blue} options**{.button .large} menu in the main menu or the pause menu.
2. Click the **:material-mailbox:{.button .item .dark-green} General**{.button .large} button to access the "General Options" menu.
3. Look for the "Advanced Camera Settings" header. Find the "Disable Camera Mode Switch Animation" option under the same header.
4. Toggle the option to your preferences.

## Moving around the camera using the keyboard

You can move around your view at fixed speeds by pressing one of the following keys:

- ++up++ - Pitches the camera up.
- ++down++ - Pitches the camera down.
- ++left++ - Turns the camera to the left.
- ++right++ - Turns the camera to the right.
