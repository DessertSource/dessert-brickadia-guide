# Avatar Editor

![Assortment of avatars](../assets/media/essentials/avatar-editor/avatars.png){.borderless}

The avatar editor allows you to customize how your character looks with tons of cosmetics and body part.
It also allows you to save your existing character as an preset.

To start off,
let's show how simple it is to use the avatar editor by making a quick avatar of Sans and saving it to our presets:

{{video("../assets/media/essentials/avatar-editor/creating-an-avatar-from-scratch","Creating an avatar from scratch")}}

This video may be good enough for you already if you are astute, but just in case here is a step by step guide below.

!!! note

    There is a search bar in the avatar editor, but it is not implemented yet.

## Finding an avatar part from your avatar

Clicking on an avatar part without doing anything else will highlight
its location.

![Clicking to see the location of the avatar part we clicked on](../assets/media/essentials/avatar-editor/avatar-editor-pick-part.jpg)

## Equipping avatar items

Clicking on any of the body parts, accessories or stickers in the catalog will equip them with their default colors automatically on your avatar.

![Clicking to equip](../assets/media/essentials/avatar-editor/avatar-editor-equip.png)

Any body parts that can be attached to the left and right of your avatar will be equipped on both sides symmetrically when you click.

If you already have one side of that part attached, the other side will still attach if you click that part.

You can also drag body parts, accessories or stickers and drop them on your avatar too.

![Click and dragging to equip](../assets/media/essentials/avatar-editor/avatar-editor-equip-drag.png)

This method allows you to asymmetrically equip a body part on a single side of your avatar.

If the outline on hover is green on one or both sides,
that means you have already equipped the body part or accessory.

![alt text](../assets/media/essentials/avatar-editor/avatar-editor-green-outline.png)

## Removing avatar items

Click and drag a part or accessory away from your avatar to remove it.

![Removing an avatar item](../assets/media/essentials/avatar-editor/avatar-editor-remove.png)

Body parts are not removed but replaced with default equivalents.

### Removing avatar stickers

Stickers cannot be removed through click and dragging, but they can be removed by clicking the "Blank" sticker pinned on the top.

![Blank sticker location in avatar editor](../assets/media/essentials/avatar-editor/avatar-editor-blank-sticker.png)

If you have both a face sticker and a torso sticker,
the torso sticker will be removed first.

## Painting your avatar

Click the brush button **:material-brush:**{.button .tiny}.
This opens up the color palette and allows you to paint the avatar.

![Avatar editor palette](../assets/media/essentials/avatar-editor/avatar-editor-palette.png)

As long as that window is open, you can paint your avatar.

Click on an avatar part to paint it. Click *and* hold on an avatar part to keep painting continously across other avatar parts.

![Avatar painted green for an amazing reason](../assets/media/essentials/avatar-editor/avatar-editor-painting.jpg)

Click the close **:material-brush:**{.button .tiny} button to close the palette window.

!!! tip

    Remember to always paint the neck and the leg joints first before adding accessories, as those are some of the most missed and troublesome parts when painting.

### Pick avatar color

Middle click a part of the avatar part to pick its color. The color in the palette that part has will be highlighted.

![Picking a color in the Avatar editor](../assets/media/essentials/avatar-editor/avatar-editor-pick-color.jpg)

## Undoing/redoing an avatar change

Avatar changes are saved to history, therefore you can undo or redo them.

- ++lctrl+z++: Undo painting or equipping/removing avatar parts
- ++lctrl+y++: Redo painting or equipping/removing avatar parts
