# Contribution

You can create pull requests to fix issues you've spotted, add new information or rephrase things so that they are better interpreted by readers.

## Resources

Everything about the site generator (Zensical) and formatting is in their documentation.

- [Zensical repository](https://github.com/zensical/zensical)
- [Zensical documentation](https://zensical.org/docs/get-started/)

## Contribution guidelines

### Keep it clear

We may ask you to change your formatting occassionally to make it easier for current and future contributors.
This also ensures consistency across the entire guide.

### Squash your commits when needed

If you are experienced with Git or software development, this is self explanatory.
For beginners, it is recommended to squash multiple commits of small changes together if the changes are marginal.
Check the following tutorials about commit squashing for your IDE of choice:

- [Visual Studio Code](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/visualstudio/version-control/git-manage-repository?view=visualstudio#merge-commits-squash)
- [IntelliJ IDEA](https://www.jetbrains.com/help/idea/edit-project-history.html#squash-commits)

### No LLM-generated content

We believe excluding LLM-generated content is a net positive for both contributors and readers, therefore it is not allowed to have LLM-generated content in your pull request or issue.
LLMs also pose a legal and capital risk on our behalf due to their improperly sourced training data without explicit formal consent and none of us have the financial backing to deal with potential lawsuits.

Any LLM use in this case, transparent or not, is at your risk.
