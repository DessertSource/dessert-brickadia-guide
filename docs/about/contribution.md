# Contribution

You can create pull requests to fix issues you've spotted, add new
information or rephrase things so that they are better interpreted
by readers.

## Resources

Everything about the site generator (Zensical) and formatting is in
their documentation.

- [Zensical repository](https://github.com/zensical/zensical)
- [Zensical documentation](https://zensical.org/docs/get-started/)
- [Zensical Studio](https://zensical.org/studio/)

## Contribution guidelines

### Keep it clear

We may ask you to change your formatting occassionally to make it
easier for current and future contributors.
This also ensures consistency across the entire guide.

### Squash your commits when needed

If you are experienced with Git or software development, this is
self explanatory. For beginners, it is recommended to squash
multiple commits of small changes together if the changes are
marginal. Check the following tutorials about commit squashing for
your IDE of choice:

- [Visual Studio Code](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/visualstudio/version-control/git-manage-repository?view=visualstudio#merge-commits-squash)
- [IntelliJ IDEA](https://www.jetbrains.com/help/idea/edit-project-history.html#squash-commits)

### LLM policy

LLM generated images are banned.

However for text, we do not always have the capability to verify
what is LLM-generated text and wish to not deploy surveillance
measures or detection tools. We cannot read minds and will not
debate about the efficiacy of LLMs for research and documentation
writing, but for the sake of preserving your own knowledge, please
do not rely entirely on LLMs to do these for you and by extension,
us.

What we can do is make you understand that we want to offer a
slop-free experience as much as sanely possible.
