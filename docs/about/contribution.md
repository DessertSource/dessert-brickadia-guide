# Contribution

You can create pull requests to fix issues you've spotted, add new
information or rephrase things so that they are better interpreted
by readers.

## Contribution guidelines

### Keep it clear

We may ask you to change your formatting occassionally to make it
easier for current and future contributors.
This also ensures consistency across the entire guide.

### Squash your commits when needed

If you are experienced with Git or software development, this is
self explanatory. For beginners, it is recommended to squash
multiple commits of small changes together if the changes are
marginal. Check the following tutorials about commit squashing for
your IDE of choice:

- [Visual Studio Code](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/visualstudio/version-control/git-manage-repository?view=visualstudio#merge-commits-squash)
- [IntelliJ IDEA](https://www.jetbrains.com/help/idea/edit-project-history.html#squash-commits)

### LLM policy

**This project disencourages the usage of large language models (LLMs) by team
members and contributors.** While there are equally valid reasons why a team of
developers refuse to use LLMs, the primary reasons we choose this policy is
outlined below:

#### Loss of context

We believe using LLMs make us forget about very important decisions taken when
developing software. While the reasons are not entirely based on scientific
objectivity, it is reasonable to expect that the user of an LLM may develop
less of a hands-on approach and check everything less often.

#### Bad quality

LLM generated visuals cause distrust amongst our users. Not only that, they are
a very contentious point of discussion, mostly towards the side of negative
sentiment.  Since we already have to make our own screenshots, assets, icons or
record videos, we do not need to rely on LLM generated visuals.
